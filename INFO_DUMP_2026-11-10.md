# INFO-DUMP — drone-arm state at 2026-11-10

Temporary context snapshot. Not a release. No RF or fabrication claim is made.

## 1. Purpose and role in the program

`drone-arm` is the arm/PCB hardware program: KiCad Rev B board, RF front-end, routing,
and fabrication evidence. The objective is a board that passes its own ERC/DRC/RF gates
and has a first-article measurement plan.

- `hardware/main/rev_b/main_rev_b.kicad_pcb` — 2.25 MB KiCad 9 board.
- `hardware/main/rev_b/main_rev_b_routed.kicad_pro` — routed project.
- `hardware/main/rev_b/rf_915.kicad_sch` — 915 MHz RF schematic.
- `diagnostics/` — copied RF/EM evidence from the working directory.

Last commit: `pcb: close RF MOSI route with replay evidence`. That is route evidence,
not RF performance evidence.

## 3. What has been tried (and what it does not prove)

### 3.1 openEMS idealized microstrip
- Straight-line microstrip runs with a PEC reference plane reached their energy stop
  criterion.
- They omit the antenna, components, real return geometry, and copper loss.
- S11 differed substantially on refinement; the runs are **not mesh-converged** and are
  not RF sign-off.

### 3.2 Gerber-derived ROI
- `rf_gerber_roi_v2` and `rf_gerber_roi_v3_refined` used a 3V3 reference plane and a
  lossless dielectric.
- Both **fail strict passive power balance at 915 MHz** (see
  `diagnostics/rf_gerber_mesh_comparison.json`).
- `rf_gerber_roi_v4_x2` added a patched constant-conductivity approximation for the FR4
  loss tangent; its run was still in progress at handoff.
- Port definitions, ROI truncation, components, antenna, and coupling remain unresolved.

### 3.3 Microstrip / via experiments
- 915 MHz and 0.1–10 GHz microstrip sweeps exist, plus solid-via experiments.
- They are diagnostic; none is a fabricator stackup solve or VNA measurement.

### 3.4 Ground / return path
- `rf_ground_audit.json` samples 11 points of the straight `/915 MHz RF/ANT_915` segment
  and records `/3V3` on In1 beneath each, and `/GND` on B.Cu.
- Point containment is **not** proof of RF return-current quality. No vias were found
  inside the specified rectangular ROI.

## 4. What has not been done

- No ERC report on this host.
- No full DRC report on this host.
- No stackup/impedance solve against a fabricator's actual materials.
- No first-article VNA/TDR measurement.
- No fabrication.
