# Bearing Specification

## Arrangement

- Front (prop end): **6901-2RS** thin-section deep groove, 12x24x6 mm, radially supports the prop overhung load and all axial thrust.
- Rear: **685-2RS** extra-thin-section, 5x11x3 mm (or 688 if shaft stiffness review requires), locates the rotor axially at the cold end.
- Both bearings pressed onto ground shaft seats and clamped in machined aluminum housings with a light axial preload wave washer at the rear.

## Loads and life

| Load case | Front bearing | Rear bearing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prop thrust 45 N axial | carries fully | negligible |
| Rotor weight plus 1g maneuver ~10 N radial | majority | remainder |
| Residual unbalanced magnetic pull (10% of mean gap attraction) | shared by span | shared by span |

L10 basic rating life target: at least 2000 h at design point loads and 13,300 rpm.
Grease: high-speed synthetic polyurea or ester, suitable to 25,000 rpm DN
envelope. -2RS contact seals retained; shielded (-ZZ) alternate only with a
manufacturer-approved relubrication plan.

## Installation

- Shaft seat tolerances per tolerances.md; housing bores H7.
- Assembly by pressing on the inner ring only; never transmit force through balls.
- Maximum assembly heating temperature for bearings: 110 C.
