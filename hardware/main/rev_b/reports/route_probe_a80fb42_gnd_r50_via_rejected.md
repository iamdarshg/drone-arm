# `/GND` plane-tie via near R50 rejected for assembly uncertainty

The temporary candidate adds one 0.60 mm / 0.30 mm through-via on `/GND` at `(111.8363,65.7208)` to bridge the isolated F.Cu GND island near R50. Source commit: `a80fb425314ae524ebcc43e25ac7af955e880555`; source PCB SHA-256 `095aa3fd44285b885c2a4ca68c05633b169dd053fbc8407d2160e8585e73336b`; candidate PCB SHA-256 `966611a9c8caa4b6bc4974038a4666de791177b1ed7cb81f35518b42c918792b`; matching project SHA-256 `f5bfbb6150d2799b8cc0ddb899c0e18c86ef452cbf68e05c60f883a969068a9e`.

I independently refilled source and candidate with KiCad `pcbnew.ZONE_FILLER.Fill(board.Zones())`, then ran KiCad CLI 9.0.2 `pcb drc --severity-all --format json` with the matching project in each board directory. The paired run reproduced **7 violations / 42 unconnected** on source and **7 / 41** on candidate with zero DRC signature changes. JSON SHA-256: source [`route_probe_a80fb42_gnd_r50_source_drc.json`](route_probe_a80fb42_gnd_r50_source_drc.json), `e59920d38c0dbabea373072086469ba7dd871f16df72e6643866ccff63b8db3d`; candidate [`route_probe_a80fb42_gnd_r50_candidate_drc.json`](route_probe_a80fb42_gnd_r50_candidate_drc.json), `dfeb2399d3672e50d785cd1fd54099e0ef54d0f46667edaceb9f644c99876521`. The removed airwire is between the F.Cu and B.Cu GND zones. The via is through-plated and the refilled board’s F.Cu and B.Cu GND zone fills both contain its center.

The candidate is **not promoted** because its location is too close to R50 for assembly confidence. R50 courtyard spans `(111.005,65.045)` to `(111.995,66.955)` mm; the via lies inside it. R50 pad 2 `/GND` copper bounding box is `(111.18,65.22)` to `(111.82,65.76)` mm; the via overlaps that copper by about `0.284` mm. The via is tented on both outer layers in PCB settings, but its 0.30 mm drill overlaps the pad’s front solder-mask aperture geometrically: the hole’s left edge is at `x=111.686` while the pad aperture reaches `x=111.82`. KiCad DRC does not report a same-net mask bridge, but this does not establish an effective mask web, via plugging/filling, paste behavior, or assembly yield. The geometry must not be treated as an acceptable via-in-pad process without explicit fabricator and assembler approval.

I inspected the small isolated F.Cu GND fill outline near R50 (filled polygon 2; about `1.392 mm²`, bounding box `(111.2434,64.8860)` to `(113.0374,66.5247)` mm) for other via locations. Within this island, a 0.60 mm via cannot maintain both at least 0.125 mm wall clearance from the polygon edge and at least 0.30 mm clearance outside the R50 courtyard. A trial move to `(112.4,66.1)` clears the courtyard and pad, but falls at the island edge and becomes a dangling via after refill: 8 violations / 42 unconnected. Its full JSON is [`route_probe_a80fb42_gnd_r50_relocated_drc.json`](route_probe_a80fb42_gnd_r50_relocated_drc.json), SHA-256 `6b5aa6dbc1203cc7e86f572efa5f921fc8d7aa25de35a2bb8526eb31704a3d86`; the relocated candidate board SHA-256 is `17717c557327ede0c4fa17e16701a62c97e82f79859f2cba4959cc267f434476`.

Conclusion: the via passes the electrical DRC gate but is rejected due unresolved pad-mask, courtyard, and assembly risks. No PCB or design expectation was changed.
